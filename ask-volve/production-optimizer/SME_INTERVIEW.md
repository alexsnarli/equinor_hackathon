# Five-minute SME check

The goal is to validate the interaction-aware forecasting angle without expanding the demo scope.

## Ask these questions

1. **Which pressure signals best describe how the wells affect one another?**
   - Exact tags, units and measurement locations
   - Whether wellhead pressure is enough or separator/manifold pressure is required

2. **When one well's choke changes, what response would you expect in the other wells?**
   - Known conflicting or complementary well combinations
   - Whether the response is immediate or delayed

3. **Are daily per-well oil, gas and water values measured directly or allocated from facility
   totals, and how reliable are the choke and pressure tags?**

4. **What would make a suggested configuration clearly plausible, impossible, or possible only
   under other conditions?**

5. **How accurate must a next-day forecast be before it is useful in the daily planning meeting?**

## Confirm the demo statement

> Given the latest measured well state and a proposed on/off and choke configuration, estimate
> next-day oil, gas and water while accounting for historical shared-pressure interactions. Show
> several options ranked by oil first and gas second, then let engineers test settings and record
> whether each prediction is operationally plausible.
