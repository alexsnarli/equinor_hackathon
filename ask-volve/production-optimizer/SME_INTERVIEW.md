# Five-minute SME check

The goal is to replace the app's placeholder operating envelope with physically meaningful limits.

## Ask these questions

1. **Which pressure decides whether a proposed lineup is feasible?**
   - Exact tag and measurement location
   - Unit
   - Minimum or maximum limit
   - Hard safety limit or preferred operating target

2. **Which facility flow constraints matter each day?**
   - Maximum gas
   - Maximum water
   - Maximum total liquid
   - Any minimum production or stability requirement

3. **Can we add the predicted well flows, or does changing one well materially change the other
   wells through shared back pressure?**

4. **What actions must the recommendation never suggest?**
   - Forbidden well combinations
   - Per-well choke minimum/maximum
   - Maximum choke change per step
   - Minimum run or shut-in time

5. **Are daily oil, gas and water values directly measured per well or allocated from facility
   totals?**

> Given the latest measured well state and a proposed on/off and choke configuration, estimate
> next-day oil, gas, water and pressure, reject configurations outside the operating envelope, and
> rank the remaining alternatives by oil first, gas second and water third.
